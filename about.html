Our Story

Over a decade ago, a single ticket to an intimate gathering changed everything. Sitting in a room of 50 people, captivated by everyday individuals sharing 10-minute slices of their lives, we experienced the undeniable power of raw, unvarnished storytelling. We left that room deeply moved, feeling far more connected to the human experience—and to each other.

As the world has grown increasingly disconnected, that need for genuine connection has only become more urgent. So That Happened was created to bridge that gap. While nothing beats the magic of a live room, the digital world allows us to bring those same powerful moments directly to you. Our mission is simple: to share real-life stories in a way that lets you step inside someone else’s shoes, relive their defining moments, and find the common threads that tie us all together.

Whether you're here to laugh, reflect, or share a story of your own, welcome to the community.
